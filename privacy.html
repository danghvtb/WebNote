Privacy Policy for MyNotes

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Overview

MyNotes is a privacy-first personal knowledge base application. All user notes, notebooks, and structure data are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage and optionally synchronized directly to your personal Google Drive account.

2. Data Access and Storage

MyNotes accesses your Google account only to read and write files within your dedicated MyNotes application folder on your Google Drive (using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope). We do not have access to any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Data Privacy

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any of your notes or personal data to any external third-party servers. All data remains exclusively on your device and your personal Google Drive account.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub repository issues.
